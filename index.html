About简介

I am a scholar of digital and ecological cultures and an Associate Professor at the School of Literature and Arts, Southwest University of Science and Technology (SWUST) in Mianyang, China.

I received my PhD in Literary Anthropology from Sichuan University in 2026, supervised by Prof. Fei Li. My dissertation listened to the work songs (haozi) of fishermen in eastern Zhejiang as oral tradition and sound narrative. In 2025–2026 I was a visiting doctoral researcher in cultural anthropology at the University of Bologna, working with Prof. Marc Brightman on a China Scholarship Council fellowship.

I study how technological systems reshape cultural difference, memory and more-than-human life. Recently I have focused especially on critical AI studies and Anthropocene studies. My scholarly aim is to help the public see the human and non-human communities that have been overlooked and pushed to the margins, and the cultures they carry.

我是西南科技大学文学与艺术学院特聘副教授，数字与生态文化研究学者。

我于2026年获四川大学文学人类学博士学位，师从李菲教授。博士论文以浙东渔民号子为对象，从口头传统与声音叙事的角度展开研究。2025年至2026年，我作为国家公派高水平项目联合培养博士研究生赴意大利博洛尼亚大学学习文化人类学，合作导师为马克·布赖特曼（Marc Brightman）教授。

我研究技术系统如何重塑文化差异、记忆与超越人类的生命。近期，我特别关注批判性人工智能研究与人类世研究，我的学术理想是带领公众看到被忽视和边缘化的人类与非人类群体及其文化。
